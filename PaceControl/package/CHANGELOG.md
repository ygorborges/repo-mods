# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: `RequiredExtractions` says how many of a level's extraction points the level actually needs. The rest are still built and still work - they are simply optional, there for anyone who wants to keep hauling for the money.
- Each extraction still asks for exactly what it always did: the level's quota comes down by the same proportion as the points no longer needed, so 3 of 5 is 60% of the quota split three ways.
- `HaulGoalPercent` on top of that, for making every extraction lighter without changing how many there are.
- The level's endgame - the scare, the lights going out, everything coming for you - waits until the truck is about to pull out, instead of firing the moment the last needed extraction is done. Optional extractions are meant to be playable. The end-of-level heal and reviving anyone dead ride along with it and are unaffected.
- The host decides and tells the room, so everyone's HUD, truck healer and end of level agree.
